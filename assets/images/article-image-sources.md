matching the existing banner dimensions. Existing images and post references are unchanged.

## Sources and download settings

Each source page identifies the photograph as free to use under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license).
Source credits are retained below. Do not imply endorsement by depicted people or brands.
Photograph licensing does not automatically grant rights to visible trademarks or identifiable people's likenesses.

Downloads use the image service URL below with
`?fit=crop&crop=entropy&fm=jpg&q=80&w=2047&h=774`.

After visual review, banners 11, 15, 18, and 26 instead use `crop=focalpoint`, `fp-x=0.5`, and `fp-z=1`.
Their `fp-y` values are `0.28`, `0.36`, `0.4`, and `0.66`, respectively, to retain the desk accessories,
team members' faces, city architecture, and mountain reflection. Other settings remain unchanged.
